Temperature Explorer

The probabilities are the softmax (sigmoid in multi-label mode) of the class logits divided by the temperature T. The predicted class, the accuracy and the confusion matrix do not change with T: only the spread of the probabilities, so the thresholds move with it. With two classes the recall at a target precision stays the same; with more classes it can move a little.

To use a temperature, set it as logit_temperature in scripts/evaluation/evaluate_checkpoint.py: class_thresholds.json is then written at that temperature, and vision_training/deployment/export_model.py builds it into the exported model when given that file.
