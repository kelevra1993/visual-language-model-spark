Temperature Explorers

The evaluations of the classifiers of the experiments folders that hold a temperature explorer. Open one to choose its temperature, save its thresholds and export it to <run>/Exports/. Evaluation "Training": <run>/Evaluation/, written by the training; "Checkpoint evaluation": <run>/Checkpoint_Evaluation/, written by evaluate_checkpoint.py. Temperatures "Any": the evaluation kept its logits, any temperature can be shown; "Grid only": the temperatures of its slider.
