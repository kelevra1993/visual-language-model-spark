## Goal
The backbone learns from images without labels: two random views of every image, drawn by the types of `Data.data_augmentation` with the self-supervised strengths (crop, flips, rotation, perspective, jigsaw, colour jitter, grayscale, gamma, haze, linear illumination, plasma shadow, Gaussian and motion blurs, solarization, Gaussian noise, black-out of grid cells, random erasing, each with its probability, the solarization and the black-out on the second view only) go through the same backbone, global pooling and projection head, and the loss pulls the two views of an image together:
- `simclr`: InfoNCE over the 2N views of the batch, every view having to find its partner among the 2N - 1 others (cosine similarities divided by a temperature, cross-entropy whose target is the partner).
- `barlow_twins`: the cross-correlation matrix of the embedding features of both views, every feature normalized over the batch, pushed to the identity (diagonal to 1 for the invariance, off-diagonal to 0 for the redundancy reduction).

The negatives of an image are the other images of its batch. With `Data.grouped_batches`, the batches gather blocks of images sharing an attribute of the optional `groups` of their sample in `labels.json` (same camera, scene or device type), so that these negatives are harder; without groups, the batches stay random.

The trained backbone then initialises the backbone of the other tasks through `Backbone.pretrained`, the projection head being left out.

## Components
- `vision_training/data/classification/classification_dataset.py`: `keep_unlabelled_samples`, keeping the images without `category_id` or of an unmapped category with `IGNORED_LABEL_INDEX`, and the optional `groups` of every sample in its record.
- `vision_training/data/grouped_batch_sampler.py`: `GroupedBatchSampler`, the grouped batches rebuilt at every pass, and `build_grouped_batch_sampler()`, giving random batches when the section is off or no sample holds its keys, and stopping when only some samples hold them.
- `vision_training/data/augmentations.py`: `DataAugmenter.create_views`, the two views of every image drawn by the types of the data augmentation with `SELF_SUPERVISED_AUGMENTATION_STRENGTHS`, the global probability ignored and the types of `SECOND_VIEW_ONLY_TYPES` (solarization, black-out) drawn for the second view only, in float32 outside the autocast, with the augmentation report of every view for the input viewer.
- `vision_training/data/loaders.py`: `get_classification_dataloaders(self_supervised=True)`, the training and validation loaders dropping their last incomplete batch, or given the grouped batch sampler of their split (`build_native_loader(batch_sampler=...)`), and a split smaller than the batch size stopping the launch.
- `vision_training/core/contracts.py`: `SelfSupervisedBatch`.
- `vision_training/losses/self_supervised_losses.py`: the InfoNCE and Barlow Twins losses and the batch metrics (positive pair accuracy, embedding standard deviation).
- `vision_training/models/self_supervised/`: `projection_head.py`, `self_supervised_model.py` and `inference_encoder.py` (the exported `image_encoder`, giving L2-normalized representations).
- `vision_training/evaluation/self_supervised/representation_metrics.py`: the k-nearest neighbour evaluation and the effective rank.
- `vision_training/trainers/self_supervised_trainer.py`: the training, the evaluation of the trained and averaged weights, and the input viewer panels ("View 1" and "View 2", each with the class of the image and the augmentation types drawn for that view).
- `vision_training/configuration_processing/self_supervised_checks.py`, the registration in the task, model, check, run name and export tables, and `vision_training/configurations/self_supervised/` (`configuration_example.yaml` on the ImageNet manifests with `barlow_twins`, `configuration_synthetic_shapes.yaml` on `~/Data/classifier_data/synthetic_shapes`).

## Open Points
- The views are drawn image by image with Python's random module, the images being transformed on their device from Python numbers: the two views of 256 images of 256 pixels took 0.7 s on the GPU of the DGX Spark while another training used it, against 2.5 s for the former kornia views.
- `Data.data_augmentation` draws the views and must be on for this task, its global probability being ignored, and its `random_crop` is drawn for every view (probability 1.0) unless it is off; the checks refuse the other cases.
- The k-nearest neighbour evaluation compares every labelled test image with the other test images (leave-one-out), not with the training images, so that the evaluation only reads the test split like the other tasks.
- The class names of the evaluation are taken from the categories of the three manifests. The samples of a multi-label manifest (`category_ids`) are read with their categories: one with a single mapped category is labelled for the k-NN evaluation, one with several mapped categories is evaluated like an unlabelled image, the k-NN classification giving one class per image.
