EVALUATION

See which classes fail, and on which images

  • Confusion matrix shaded by the share of every true class.
  • Precision and recall in its margins.
  • Misclassified images saved per pair of classes, named with their probability.
  • A temperature explorer sets the thresholds of a target precision.

Rectangle read as square, trapezoid as parallelogram, hollow parallelogram as hollow trapezoid.

To find where a classifier fails, read its confusion matrix first: every cell counts the images of its true class given its predicted class, shaded by their share of the true class, with the recall in the right margin and the precision in the bottom one. Then open the misclassified images, saved in Errors/<true>-TO-<predicted>/ with a maximum per pair of classes, each named after the sample and the probability of the predicted class. Here the synthetic shapes classifier at iteration 2000 reads rectangles as squares (574 of 1,483) and trapezoids as parallelograms (614 of 1,387), both among the first 13 of its 35 classes shown, and hollow parallelograms as hollow trapezoids (631 of 1,382), the three pairs of the error folders. When a class reaches its target precision only above a very high threshold, the temperature explorer picks a logit temperature and saves the thresholds of every target precision for the export.
