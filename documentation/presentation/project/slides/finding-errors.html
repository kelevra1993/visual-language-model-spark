EVALUATION

See which classes fail, and on which images

  • Confusion matrix in percent of every true class.
  • Precision and recall in its margins.
  • Misclassified images saved per pair of classes, named with their probability.
  • A temperature explorer sets the thresholds of a target precision.

Square read as rectangle, circle as octagon, pentagon as hexagon.

To find where a classifier fails, read its confusion matrix first: every row is in percent of its true class, with the recall in the right margin and the precision in the bottom one. Then open the misclassified images, saved in Errors/<true>-TO-<predicted>/ with a maximum per pair of classes, each named after the sample and the probability of the predicted class. Here the synthetic shapes classifier at iteration 1500 confuses squares with rectangles, circles with octagons and pentagons with hexagons, and the error images show small shapes. When a class reaches its target precision only above a very high threshold, the temperature explorer picks a logit temperature and saves the thresholds of every target precision for the export.
