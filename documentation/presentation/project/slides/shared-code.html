ARCHITECTURE

One training loop, one trainer per task

  • base_trainer.py runs the loop of every task.
  • The task named in the configuration picks its trainer.
  • That trainer fills the hooks: data, model, losses, evaluation.

A new task brings its dataset, head, losses and metrics, not a new loop.

Point at the two columns. On the left, what every task shares in base_trainer.py: reading the next batch, augmenting it on the GPU, the backward pass and optimizer step with the learning rate schedule, the moving average of the weights and the checkpoints, and the reports to Results.txt, TensorBoard and MLflow. On the right, the hooks each task fills in its own trainers/<task>_trainer.py: building its data loaders and model, preparing a batch, the forward pass with its losses, and the prediction and metrics at evaluation. The trainer is picked from ExperimentConfiguration.task by vision_training/core/task_registry.py. Adding a task means writing its dataset, head, losses and metrics, while the loop, checkpoints and reports come for free.
