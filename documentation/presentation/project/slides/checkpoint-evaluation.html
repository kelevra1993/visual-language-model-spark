EVALUATION

Score any checkpoint again, after training

  • On another split, batch size or final NMS.
  • With “all”, every checkpoint is compared and the best is printed.
  • Written to its own folder, the training files untouched.
  • Report figures redrawn by a second script.
scripts/evaluation/evaluate_checkpoint.py scores the checkpoints of a trained run again: on another labelled split, with another batch size, or for detection and instance segmentation with another final NMS. Its iteration is None for the last checkpoint, a number, or “all”, in which case evaluation_summary.csv holds one row per checkpoint and the best one is printed. Everything goes into <run>/Checkpoint_Evaluation/, the Weights, Evaluation and Results.txt of the training staying untouched; save_predictions writes predictions.csv with every evaluated sample, and a classifier keeps class_logits.npz for its temperature explorer. scripts/evaluation/draw_report_figures.py redraws the figures of a research report: confusion matrix, precision-recall curves, training curves with the kept checkpoint, and the numbers they show.
