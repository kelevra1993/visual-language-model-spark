EVALUATION

Cluster similar frames and keep a few of each

Made for datasets of video frames, many of them nearly identical.

  • Embeds every image with a trained run.
  • Clusters them: HDBSCAN, DBSCAN or k-means after a PCA.
  • Writes the clusters, their mosaics and the images to keep.

Here: 720 synthetic shapes, grouped into 5 clusters and 6 isolated images.

The script scripts/embedding/cluster_image_embeddings.py runs the images through a trained model, takes the features of the layers you ask for, and clusters them with HDBSCAN, DBSCAN or k-means after a PCA. For every layer it writes the clusters, a mosaic per cluster and three folders of kept images to compare: chosen by the clustering, drawn at random, and spread evenly over the groups. The example is 720 synthetic shapes: the clusters follow what the classifier layer separates, squares with rectangles, the four round shapes together. The stored features are reused when the script is launched again.
