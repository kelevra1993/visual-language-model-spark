QUALITY

Tests guard the code, videos onboard newcomers

870 tests pass on main in about 6 minutes, short trainings of every task included.

35 narrated chapters in 12 series, about 4 hours, their numbers read from real runs and the code.

The tests mirror the code folders, so a change to one folder is checked by running its mirror alone; they include a short training of every task on a tiny synthetic dataset. 870 tests pass on the current main in about six minutes; the figure counts the 854 cases of 15 November. The narrated tutorials, an overview, a foundations series and one series per task, are rebuilt from storyboards kept in the repository: every number on screen is read at build time from real runs, configuration files or the code, and every quoted file, function and setting is checked against the repository. The index page gives one player per series with its chapters and scenes as links.
