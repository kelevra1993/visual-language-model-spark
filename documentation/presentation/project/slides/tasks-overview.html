One evaluation sample of every supervised task, each from a short run on its synthetic dataset. Top row: classification writes the true class, then the predicted one with its score (“hexagon -> hexagon”); detection and instance segmentation draw the ground truth on the left and the prediction on the right, with their scores. Middle row: the keypoints of five shapes (octagon, square, hollow pentagon, star, hollow kite), the semantic segmentation input, ground truth, prediction and errors, and a sequence of frames classified as “moving up”. Bottom row: sequence labelling frame by frame, a before-and-after comparison, and frame selection, where the chosen frames (largest circle, triangle and star) are framed in green. Self-supervised pre-training is not in the grid: it predicts no labels, it prepares the backbone of the others.
