EVALUATION

A gap counts only beyond the launch spread

Repeated squeeze-and-excitation stage: six launches per setting overlap, means 0.21 points apart for a standard error of 1.11 points.

After the launches of a hyperparameter stage have trained, three scripts of scripts/evaluation/ compare them: collect_experiment_results.py gathers the test curves and swept settings into one CSV with a mean row per setting, compare_best_evaluations.py prints Markdown tables of the best test evaluation of every run, over the trained and the averaged weights, and plot_experiment_comparisons.py draws one figure per stage. The rule: compare a gap with the spread of the launches of each setting before trusting it. In the repeated squeeze-and-excitation stage on the right, six launches per setting overlap, and the means differ by 0.21 points for a standard error of 1.11 points, so the difference is noise.
