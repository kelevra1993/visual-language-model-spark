AT A GLANCE

One framework to train, evaluate and export

10

vision tasks on one backbone design

870

automated tests, short trainings of every task included

35

narrated tutorial chapters, about 4 hours

Train

One YAML file per run, checked before launch; resumes on its own after a stop.

Evaluate

Per-class reports, confusion matrices, error images, launch comparisons and MLflow.

Export

TorchScript and ONNX checked against PyTorch, served by Triton with TensorRT.

The three verbs of the repository: train, evaluate, export. Numbers: 10 tasks, 870 tests passing on the current main, 35 tutorial chapters (about 243 minutes of narrated video). The ten tasks are the headline: every one of them uses the same backbone design, each training its own weights, and shares the loop, the configuration format, the reports and the export.
