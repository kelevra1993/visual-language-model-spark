AT A GLANCE

One framework to train, evaluate and export

10

vision tasks on one backbone design

870

automated tests, short trainings of every task included

35

narrated tutorial chapters, one short series per task

Train

One YAML file per run, checked before launch; resumes on its own after a stop.

Evaluate

Per-class reports, confusion matrices, error images, launch comparisons and MLflow.

Export

TorchScript and ONNX checked against PyTorch, served by Triton with TensorRT.

Tutorials: start with the overview (4 min) and the foundations (38 min), then watch only your task:
CLS 21 · DET 29 · INS 15 · KPT 19 · SEG 18 · M2O 19 · M2M 23 · CMP 15 · SEL 19 · SSL 21 minutes

The three verbs of the repository: train, evaluate, export. Numbers: 10 tasks, 870 tests passing on the current main, 35 tutorial chapters. Nobody watches all of them: a newcomer watches the 4-minute overview, the 38 minutes of foundations, then only the series of their task, 15 to 29 minutes each (about 243 minutes in all, if one watched everything). The ten tasks are the headline: every one of them uses the same backbone design, each training its own weights, and shares the loop, the configuration format, the reports and the export.
