Train

One YAML file per run, checked before launch; resumes on its own after a stop.

Evaluate

Per-class reports, confusion matrices, error images, launch comparisons and MLflow.

Export

TorchScript and ONNX checked against PyTorch, served by Triton with TensorRT.

The three verbs of the repository: train, evaluate, export. Numbers: 10 tasks, 870 tests passing on the current main, 35 tutorial chapters (about 243 minutes of narrated video), 1,110 commits since the first one on 17 August 2026.
