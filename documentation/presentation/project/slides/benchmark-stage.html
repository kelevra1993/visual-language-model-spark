The architecture benchmarks of documentation/architecture_benchmarking hold four experiment plans, classification, object detection, instance segmentation and sequence labelling, and 41 stage figures. Every setting is launched twice and scored on the averaged weights at the last iteration of the stage; a challenger whose gap is smaller than the gap between the two launches of either setting is a tie, and the earlier setting stays. Detection stage 7, on screen: per-level proposal selection reaches 81.83 % against 80.79 % test mAP@[0.5:0.95] at 15,000 iterations, +1.04 points, beyond the 0.65-point gap between its two launches, so it was kept and set in the detection configurations. Instance segmentation: the stage 0 reference reads 78.84 % mask mAP, the mean of two launches at 15,000 with the averaged weights; in stage 1 a RoIAlign pool of 22 reads 77.61 %, −1.22 points, beyond its launch gaps, so the pool of 14 was kept.
