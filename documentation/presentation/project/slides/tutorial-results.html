Synthetic test sets, runs of the narrated tutorials kept short on purpose: they show that every pipeline works, not the best score a task can reach.

Each row is the test result of the run that the tutorial series of that task narrates, on its synthetic shapes test set. The runs are deliberately short, a few thousand iterations, so that the videos can be rebuilt; they prove that data, training, evaluation and reports work for every task, not what a long training reaches. A few secondary numbers: the classifier’s macro F1 is 82.73 %, the keypoint detector’s mean OKS 94.30 %, the semantic segmenter’s pixel accuracy 97.60 %. The sequence labeller predicts 71 classes per frame, so its macro F1 of 67.49 % is lower than its frame accuracy. The self-supervised run is scored by a k-NN classifier on its embeddings, against a chance level of 11.11 % for nine shapes.
