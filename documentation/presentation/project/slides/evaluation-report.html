TRAINING

Every evaluation writes a per-class report

  • Classes worst first, recall with its 95 % interval.
  • Rates in percent, changes in points.
  • TensorBoard: Train, Validation, Test, Test_EMA.
At every evaluation the run prints this report and appends it to Results.txt: the global metrics, the per-class metrics and, with evaluation.per_class_report, the classes worst first with their recall and its 95 % confidence interval. Rates are written as percentages (“94.03 %”) and their changes in points (“+0.52 points”), while the files, TensorBoard and MLflow keep the values from 0 to 1. TensorBoard holds Train and Validation at every iteration, Test with sample images at every evaluation, and Test_EMA for the averaged weights. The screenshot is the synthetic shapes classifier at iteration 2000, scored on 50,000 test images, up to its per-class table of the 35 classes: the weak classes (trapezoid, rhombus, hollow trapezoid) are visible at a glance at the top.
