The three numbers come from run_inference.py on the README classifier at 512 pixels, one client sending one request at a time, measured once on 23 October while three trainings shared the GPU: lower than the model gives alone, and to be measured again on an idle GPU. TensorRT FP32 gave 96.59 images per second, twice the eager PyTorch model in the process and 2.2 times TorchScript through Triton. TensorRT fuses the convolution, batch normalization and ReLU of every block into one kernel and picks the fastest kernels for this GPU. FP16 can be exported but the served engine is not checked against PyTorch; BF16, INT8 and FP8 are not offered yet.
